1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

2. Cookies We Use

Chinese Name Generator Male uses a minimal approach to cookies:

2.1 Essential Local Storage

We use your browser's local storage (not traditional cookies) solely for the purpose of tracking daily usage limits. This includes:

  • A date stamp to identify the current day
  • A count of how many names you've requested today

This information is stored locally on your device only. It is never sent to our servers or shared with third parties.

2.2 No Tracking Cookies

We do NOT use:

  • Analytics cookies to track your browsing behavior
  • Advertising cookies to serve targeted ads
  • Third-party tracking services
  • Cookies to identify individual users across websites

3. How We Use Local Storage

The data we store locally is used only to:

  • Enforce our daily generation limits
  • Provide a fair service to all users
  • Prevent abuse of our free service

It contains no personal information and cannot be used to identify you.

4. Managing Cookies and Local Storage

You can manage or delete local storage and cookies through your browser settings. Here's how for common browsers:

4.1 Google Chrome

  • Go to Settings → Privacy and security → Clear browsing data
  • Select "Cookies and other site data"
  • Click "Clear data"

4.2 Firefox

  • Go to Settings → Privacy & Security → Clear Data
  • Check "Cookies and Site Data"
  • Click "Clear"

4.3 Safari

  • Go to Settings → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Search for our website and remove data

4.4 Microsoft Edge

  • Go to Settings → Privacy, search, and services → Clear browsing data
  • Select "Cookies and other site data"
  • Click "Clear now"

5. Disabling Local Storage

If you disable local storage, our daily usage limit feature will not work correctly. You may still be able to use the basic name generation features, but generation may be limited or unavailable.

6. Third-Party Cookies

Our website may embed content from third parties (such as Google Fonts) that may set their own cookies. We have no control over these third-party cookies. Please review their privacy policies for more information.

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We will notify you of changes by updating the "Last Updated" date of this policy.
