Elven Naming Traditions

Elves in fantasy have always had that thing about nature, stars, and ancient wisdom. Names like what Tolkien used and stuff you see in modern fantasy tend to sound melodic and elegant. Our name generator for elves takes inspiration from all that.

Guy elf names usually end in consonants or suffixes like -on, -or, -ion. Girl elf names often end in -iel, -ith, -a, or -ien - gives them that graceful, flowing vibe. The name generator for elves keeps all this in mind when spinning up new names.
