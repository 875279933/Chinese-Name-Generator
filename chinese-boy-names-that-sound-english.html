These names bridge two cultures beautifully—they sound familiar to English speakers while carrying Chinese cultural depth. Perfect for families with international connections or anyone wanting a name that works globally.

When selecting, consider how the name flows with the surname and its meaning. Many of these names incorporate characters with positive connotations like peace, kindness, and wisdom.
