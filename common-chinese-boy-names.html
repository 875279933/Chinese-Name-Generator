Common Chinese boy names are popular for good reason—they're beautiful, meaningful, and culturally significant. Whether you prefer a modern trendy name or a timeless classic, these names have been loved by generations of families.

When selecting a common name, consider how it sounds with the surname and what qualities you want to convey. Many of these names work well across different ages and life stages, making them versatile choices.
